# Manual save list — DarGlobal (human browsing, no automation)

Why: darglobal.co.uk serves an Imperva bot challenge to automated clients. The scraper stopped (SPEC §6.3).
These pages are public and allowed by robots.txt; a person opens them in a normal browser and saves them.
The pipeline only parses the saved files offline. Nothing is bypassed.

## How (Chrome or Edge)
1. Open the URL, wait until the page fully loads, scroll once to the bottom (loads lazy sections).
2. `Ctrl+S` → Save as type **"Webpage, Complete"** → save into this folder
   (`C:\Users\intel\Downloads\OU\projects\dar\darglobal-concierge\scraper\manual\darglobal\`).
   Keep the default filename.
3. Skip any page that shows an error or a "verify you are human" screen. Don't solve challenges for it.

Do the **Priority A** pages first. Even 15 pages give a strong DarGlobal corpus.
